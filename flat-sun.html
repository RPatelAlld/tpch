    Direct sun on each outside wall, 21st of the month. Pick a month:

    How this is worked out: tower and flat positions are traced from the brochure floor plates and masterplan and checked against the approved TRERA site plan. Towers are 134.15 m tall; true north is set from satellite imagery. The sun is calculated for the site (17.31° N, 78.42° E) on the 21st of each month, in 10-minute steps, and a wall counts as sunlit when sun reaches most of it without another tower in the way. Balcony slabs, fins, trees and the upcoming mall are not modelled, and side walls facing a narrow gap between flats are shown even though their windows see little sky. Refuge floors (19, 29, 39) have a different layout. Treat times as ±15 minutes, and check against your unit plan. This is an owners' tool, not a Prestige document.
