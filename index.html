These insights belong to the owners who built them. Add your own flat to the Registry, and you unlock everyone else's — aggregated, never individual.

1 · Add your flat

A 60-second form — tower, facing, and the figures from the grey box on your cost sheet. Your individual entry is never shown to anyone.

2 · Join the owners' group

You get an automated email with an access link. Sign in with the Google account you used on the form — that's what unlocks the members-only tools.

3 · See everyone's

Reciprocal: because you shared yours, you now see the community aggregates — price benchmarks, plans and demand. Only ever totals and ranges, never who-paid-what.
