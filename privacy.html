Privacy

Your data, handled with care.

A plain-English note on what this owner-run Hub collects, why, and what is never shown to anyone. In short: individual entries stay private — the public only ever sees aggregated totals.

Last updated: September 2026

Who runs this

The TPCH Owners Hub (tpch.in) is an independent, owner-run community resource for residents of The Prestige City, Hyderabad. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Prestige Group or Vaishnaoi Group. Questions or requests: rpatel.alld@gmail.com or the Owners' WhatsApp group.

What we collect

  • The Owners' Registry. When you fill our registry form (a Google Form), we store what you enter — your name, email, tower/unit, configuration, the cost-sheet figures and preferences you choose to share — in a private Google Sheet that only the Hub organisers can open.
  • Sign in with Google. When you use the “Sign in with Google” button on the Community Demand section, Google shares your email address and basic profile (name, picture) with us. We never see your Google password.
  • Basic analytics. We use Google Analytics (GA4) for aggregate visit statistics — pages viewed, approximate region, device type. This does not identify you individually.

How “Sign in with Google” is used

We use your Google email for one purpose: to find your entry in the Owners' Registry and open your own demand list so you can add and edit it. That's it. We store your email as your key in the Registry/Owners records; we do not retain your Google profile picture or use your Google account for anything else.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We request only the basic email and profile scopes, and we do not transfer or sell this data.

What is public — and what is never public

The community demand board shows only aggregated totals — quantity by category and when it's needed — so we can approach vendors with real numbers. It never shows who wants what, and the Registry's cost figures are never published individually or tied to any name. Your individual demand items are visible only to you, through your own link.

Sharing

Your individual data stays within the small team of owner-organisers who run the Hub. We may share aggregated totals (never personal details) with vendors to negotiate group rates. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Your choices & deletion

To see, correct, or delete your data — your Registry entry and any demand rows — email rpatel.alld@gmail.com and we'll remove it. You can also stop using “Sign in with Google” at any time; it stores nothing beyond your email as described above.

Changes

We may update this page as the Hub evolves; the “last updated” date above reflects the current version.
