Collective asks · before handover

What we're asking Prestige to lock down now.

Gates, metering, EV points, cabling — some things can only be set while the towers are still under construction. Once we take handover, they're effectively fixed. With 500+ owners here, a single united, documented ask carries far more weight than a message lost to the scroll. These are the items the group has raised most often. They're owner concerns, not confirmed facts — each is something we want Prestige to clarify or commit in writing before handover.

🚶
A walkable entry to the mall from the society

A direct pedestrian connection between the society and the mall — so a quick trip doesn't mean driving out one gate and back in through another. A dedicated walkway or pedestrian gate is far easier to plan into the layout now than to add after handover.

Clarity pending ⚠ Fixed at construction stage
🔌
EV charging & electrical provisioning

Conduits and points for EV charging in the parking — and the wiring for smart / prepaid meters — are far cheaper to lay during construction. Retrofitting the parking and shafts later is disruptive, expensive, and often needs association-wide approval.

Clarity pending ⚠ Fixed at construction stage
🎥
CCTV & security infrastructure

Community-wide camera coverage and cabling is much cheaper to build in now than to retrofit. Owners want to know whether Prestige provides it, or whether it becomes a large post-handover cost on the association.

Clarity pending
🔋
DG power backup — scope & cost

The brochure mentions 100% DG backup at extra cost. Owners want the exact scope, and the per-flat cost, confirmed in writing before handover rather than discovered on the first bill.

In brochure · confirmation pending
🌳
Keep the green, pedestrian-first spaces green

Many of us chose TPCH for the green podium in the renders and scale model. The RERA-approved plan can't be structurally redrawn — so the ask is two-fold: written clarity on what greenery and vehicle-free zones are actually committed, and the non-structural touches (planting, bollards, signage) that protect the pedestrian feel.

Clarity pending Design is RERA-locked — seek clarity, not redesign
